Projects

Throughout his active years, KossarSherzad created several projects combining software development and Islamic education.

DarwazayIslam

DarwazayIslam is an Islamic knowledge platform designed to provide educational resources and information for Kurdish-speaking users. The platform focused on collecting and sharing Islamic content in a digital environment.

Jiani Hawsargiri

Jiani Hawsargiri is an Android application focused on Islamic marriage education, providing information about marriage and family life according to Islamic teachings.

Aqiqa Rules

Aqiqa Rules was an application created to provide information about Aqiqa practices, including related rules, timing, and educational information.

Darwazay Islam Operation

Darwazay Islam Operation was a technology system designed for Android, Windows, and web platforms. It allowed users to create, share, and manage Islamic content inside a community environment.

The project represented an attempt to combine software development with digital community building.

DarwazayIslam Community

The DarwazayIslam Community was created as a digital space where users could share Islamic posts, educational materials, and media.

Its main purpose was connecting users through beneficial content and community interaction.
