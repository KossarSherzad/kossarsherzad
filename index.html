KossarSherzad Sheikh Mohammed, known as KossarSherzad, is an Iraqi Kurdish developer, programmer, and founder of Darwazay Islam. He is known for creating digital platforms and software projects focused on technology, education, and Islamic knowledge.

He is the founder of Darwazay Islam, a digital project created to organize and share Islamic knowledge through modern technology.

Early life and youth [edit]

KossarSherzad Sheikh Mohammed, known as KossarSherzad, was born on 16 June 2002 in Erbil, Kurdistan Region, Iraq. He grew up in a religious family environment that influenced many aspects of his early life and personal development.

His family background was connected with religious values and figures. His father was described as a religious person who supported his family, and his uncle Shahid Khider Kosary was an important figure in his family history. Khider Kosary, also known as Khider Mohammad Rasheed, was a Kurdish poet known for his writings and involvement in Kurdish Islamist movements. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1} The memory and influence associated with Shahid Khider Kosary became connected with KossarSherzad's identity, including the origin of his name. Growing up in a family with religious and historical influences contributed to his early interest in knowledge, ideas, and learning.

During his childhood, KossarSherzad showed strong curiosity toward technology and computers. From an early age, he was interested in understanding how computers worked, including both hardware and software. He wanted to learn how digital systems were created and how technology could be used to solve problems.

As a child and teenager, he was described as quiet, curious, and creative. Rather than focusing only on entertainment, he often explored new ideas and tried to understand complex subjects. His curiosity about technology gradually developed into a deeper interest in programming and software development.

Reading was also an important part of his early intellectual development. He had an interest in history, strategy, and different ideas. Among the books and writings that influenced his thinking were works related to Mulla Krekar. He also developed an interest in strategic thinking and historical events.

Education and school years

During his school years, KossarSherzad developed an interest in science and technical subjects. However, he later described this period as a challenging stage of his life, as he felt distant from his religious values during that time and was influenced by his surroundings.

a significant change happened around the age of 20 when he met a friend named Abdullah, whom he considered an important influence in his return toward religious life. Their friendship continued for several years and became an important part of his personal development and spiritual journey. Abdullah later passed away in 2023, after approximately three years of friendship with KossarSherzad. During this period, KossarSherzad also experienced events that affected his personal life and public activities. In 2019, he was reportedly imprisoned for a short period in connection with his activist activities and was released after one day. Another reported imprisonment occurred in 2024 in the Kurdistan Region; the exact reason for this event remains unclear, and he was released after approximately one day. These experiences became part of the events surrounding KossarSherzad's later years and contributed to his increasingly private lifestyle and reduced public activity.

After this change, KossarSherzad became interested in using his abilities and technical skills for purposes connected with his religious beliefs. He focused on learning, creating projects, and using technology as a way to support education and knowledge sharing.

First interest in computers and programming

KossarSherzad's first access to a computer came through his father's computer. He used it as an opportunity to explore technology, learn how computers worked, and understand software systems.

His interest in programming developed from a desire to understand and create technology rather than only use it. He believed that skills and knowledge should be used for meaningful purposes and that technology could become a tool for serving ideas and communities.

Strategic thinking and problem solving

KossarSherzad became known among people around him for his problem solving ability and strategic style of thinking. His interest in strategy was influenced partly by strategy-based games and his interest in history and planning.

During school and later work experiences, friends and colleagues often turned to him when they faced difficult problems. He became recognized among his circle as someone who could analyze situations and search for solutions.

Childhood accident

During his childhood, KossarSherzad experienced a serious accident when he was approximately eight to ten years old. According to his account, he was behind a vehicle when his father reversed the car without knowing he was there, causing him to fall under the vehicle. He survived the incident, which he considers an important event in his life.

Professional career [edit]

Before focusing mainly on software development and digital projects, KossarSherzad gained experience in business management and leadership through his work in the retail sector.

At the age of 18, KossarSherzad began working with Teammart , a retail company operating in Kurdistan Region, Iraq.

During his time at Teammart, he took responsibility in management roles, including working as a market manager for approximately two years and later as a supervisor for approximately one year.

His experience in retail management introduced him to organizational leadership, employee coordination, customer service, inventory control, and solving daily operational challenges inside a large market environment.

Managing a retail environment at a young age became an important part of his personal and professional development. It helped him improve skills such as decision-making, planning, communication, and problem-solving.

During this period, he became interested in strategic thinking and finding efficient solutions for complex situations. The experience of organizing people, resources, and daily operations later influenced the way he approached software projects and system development.

The combination of business management experience and technical knowledge became one of the characteristics associated with his working style. He approached both business problems and programming challenges through planning, analysis, and long-term thinking.

Education and learning [edit]

During his education, KossarSherzad continued learning through both school and personal study. At one stage of his education, he was a student in the fifth grade of school.

Alongside formal education, he spent time reading books and exploring different areas of knowledge.

Influences and interests [edit]

Throughout his life, KossarSherzad developed interests in reading, learning, strategy, and understanding different ideas. Books played an important role in shaping his thinking style and his approach to knowledge.

Among the figures who influenced his personal interests was Mulla Krekar , whom KossarSherzad considered an important inspiration. His interest in different thinkers and discussions contributed to his analytical and strategic way of thinking.

These influences, combined with his interest in technology and programming, shaped his approach to creating digital projects and developing systems focused on education and knowledge sharing.

Programming career [edit]

Around 2015, KossarSherzad began learning programming and exploring software development.

He worked with technologies including Java, C#, Firebase, Android development, and web development.

His goal was to use technology to create useful systems and digital platforms.

Recognition and nickname [edit]

Although KossarSherzad has maintained a relatively private public profile, he became known among people familiar with his work for his ability to develop software across multiple platforms and for his methodical approach to solving technical problems. Rather than focusing on a single programming language or development environment, he pursued projects that combined mobile applications, desktop software, websites, and online services into one connected ecosystem.

Among developers and friends who followed his work, KossarSherzad developed a reputation for carefully analyzing complex technical problems before attempting to solve them. His approach emphasized understanding the root cause of a problem, researching possible solutions, and continuously learning new technologies through experimentation and practical work.

In 2024, KossarSherzad participated in discussions within the Reddit community involving an Android-based Point of Sale (POS) system. During these discussions, he contributed ideas toward solving a complex software problem while working from his Android phone. People familiar with his work later described the event as an example of his analytical approach and ability to work effectively with limited resources.

This experience reflected a broader philosophy that guided much of his work. He believed that successful software development depended more on logical thinking, persistence, and understanding than on expensive equipment or powerful computers. Whether using a desktop computer or a mobile phone, he considered knowledge and determination to be the most valuable tools of a developer.

The nickname "The Islamic Spider" became associated with KossarSherzad because of the way he connected different software projects into one digital ecosystem. Friends and collaborators used the nickname to describe his ability to work on several technologies simultaneously while keeping them interconnected, similar to the structure of a spider's web.

The nickname became especially connected with the development of Darwazay Islam and the broader Darwazay Islam Operation project. Working independently, KossarSherzad designed and developed an Android application using Java and XML, created a website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and developed a Windows desktop application using C#.

Instead of treating these as separate projects, he designed them to work together as different parts of one larger platform. The Android application, desktop software, and website were intended to complement one another by sharing ideas, workflows, and long-term development goals. This integrated approach became one of the defining characteristics of his programming style.

People who knew KossarSherzad often described him as a strategic thinker. Alongside programming, he had a strong interest in history, strategy, planning, and problem-solving. These interests influenced both the design of his software and the methods he used to organize complex projects.

Throughout his development journey, KossarSherzad expressed the belief that technical knowledge should be used to create projects that educate, organize information, and benefit communities. This philosophy influenced the design of Darwazay Islam and many of his other software projects.

Although his public activity later became limited, his software projects continue to reflect his interest in combining technology, education, and community service. The nickname "The Islamic Spider" remains associated with his ability to connect multiple technologies into a single system, symbolizing the interconnected nature of his work across Android, Windows, and the web.

Projects and works [edit]

Throughout his development journey, KossarSherzad created and contributed to several software projects across Android, web, and desktop platforms. His projects covered different fields, including Islamic education, business management, sports information systems, and organization-based software solutions.

His development approach focused on creating practical applications that solved real-world problems. Many of his projects were designed for specific users, organizations, and communities, combining software development with educational, business, and organizational needs.

Darwazay Islam

Darwazay Islam is an Islamic knowledge platform created to organize and share educational content through digital technology.

The project represents KossarSherzad's goal of combining programming, web development, and educational content into one platform. It focuses on making Islamic information accessible through digital systems.

Darwazay Islam Operation

Darwazay Islam Operation was a software system designed across multiple platforms, including Android, Windows, and web technologies.

The project included an Android application, a Windows management system, and connected digital services. It was designed to organize content, manage information, and support a community-based platform.

The Android application was developed using Java and XML , while the Windows application was developed using C# .

UAFA Football

UAFA Football was an Android application developed in 2023 for the Kurdistan UAFA Football Organization.

The application was designed as a football information platform that provided player statistics, match information, goals, and football data.

The system allowed users to view information about players, matches, and statistics, creating a digital solution for organizing football information.

UAFA Football was developed using Android Studio and Java .

The application was published through APKPure.

Afarin Operations

Afarin Operations was an Android business management application developed in 2023 for Afarin Stationery.

Unlike publicly released applications, the system was created for internal use by employees and members of the stationery business.

The application included several business management features, including:

  • Creating labels for products
  • Barcode generation
  • Barcode scanning for checking item prices
  • Stock management and quantity tracking
  • Employee fingerprint check-in and check-out system
  • Mini cashier system
  • Point of Sale (POS) calculator

The application was developed using Android, Java , and XML .

The project was completed and used for managing daily operations. Later, in 2026, Afarin Stationery closed due to reduced customer activity.

Aqiqa Rules

Aqiqa Rules was an Android educational application developed in 2025.

The application was designed as a small digital guide providing basic information about the Islamic practice of Aqiqah. Its goal was to make information accessible through a simple and lightweight mobile format.

The application was designed like a small handbook that users could read anywhere. It focused on simplicity, accessibility, and presenting information in an organized way.

Aqiqa Rules was developed using Java and XML .

The application was completed and published. Later, it was removed from Google Play after a review decision related to content inside the application.

Jiani Hawsargiri

Jiani Hawsargiri is an Android educational application developed in 2026.

The application provides educational information about marriage in Islam, helping individuals and couples understand the responsibilities, values, and purpose of married life according to Islamic teachings.

The project represents the combination of software development and educational content, using technology as a way to provide accessible learning resources.

Jiani Hawsargiri was developed using Java and XML , and was published on Google Play Store.

OBRC Training

OBRC Training was an Android application project developed in 2025 for employee training purposes.

The application was designed to help train new captains and employees on how to use the OCaptain application and understand operational procedures.

The project was developed using Android, Java , and XML .

Development of the project was later stopped after changes were made to the planned system, including the removal of the coaches section.
