KossarSherzad Shekh Mohamed, known by the nickname "The Islamic Spider", is described as an Islamic developer and technology creator focused on digital platforms, education, and community systems.


He began programming around 2015 and became known among his followers for his problem-solving abilities, creative thinking, and interest in combining technology with Islamic knowledge.


One of his most important achievements was founding DarwazayIslam, a digital platform focused on Islamic information and educational resources.
