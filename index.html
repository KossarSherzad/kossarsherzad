KossarSherzad Sheikh Mohammed, known as KossarSherzad, is an Iraqi Kurdish developer, programmer, and founder of Darwazay Islam. He is known for creating digital platforms and software projects focused on technology, education, and Islamic knowledge.

He is the founder of Darwazay Islam, a digital project created to organize and share Islamic knowledge through modern technology.

Early life and youth [edit]

KossarSherzad Sheikh Mohammed, known as KossarSherzad, was born on 16 June 2002 in Erbil, Kurdistan Region, Iraq. He grew up in a religious family environment that influenced many aspects of his early life and personal development.

His family background was connected with religious values and figures. His father was described as a religious person who supported his family, and his uncle Shahid Khider Kosary was an important figure in his family history. Khider Kosary, also known as Khider Mohammad Rasheed, was a Kurdish poet known for his writings and involvement in Kurdish Islamist movements. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1} The memory and influence associated with Shahid Khider Kosary became connected with KossarSherzad's identity, including the origin of his name. Growing up in a family with religious and historical influences contributed to his early interest in knowledge, ideas, and learning.

During his childhood, KossarSherzad showed strong curiosity toward technology and computers. From an early age, he was interested in understanding how computers worked, including both hardware and software. He wanted to learn how digital systems were created and how technology could be used to solve problems.

As a child and teenager, he was described as quiet, curious, and creative. Rather than focusing only on entertainment, he often explored new ideas and tried to understand complex subjects. His curiosity about technology gradually developed into a deeper interest in programming and software development.

Reading was also an important part of his early intellectual development. He had an interest in history, strategy, and different ideas. Among the books and writings that influenced his thinking were works related to Mulla Krekar. He also developed an interest in strategic thinking and historical events.

Education and school years

During his school years, KossarSherzad developed an interest in science and technical subjects. However, he later described this period as a challenging stage of his life, as he felt distant from his religious values during that time and was influenced by his surroundings.

a significant change happened around the age of 20 when he met a friend named Abdullah, whom he considered an important influence in his return toward religious life. Their friendship continued for several years and became an important part of his personal development and spiritual journey. Abdullah later passed away in 2023, after approximately three years of friendship with KossarSherzad. During this period, KossarSherzad also experienced events that affected his personal life and public activities. In 2019, he was reportedly imprisoned for a short period in connection with his activist activities and was released after one day. Another reported imprisonment occurred in 2024 in the Kurdistan Region; the exact reason for this event remains unclear, and he was released after approximately one day. These experiences became part of the events surrounding KossarSherzad's later years and contributed to his increasingly private lifestyle and reduced public activity.

After this change, KossarSherzad became interested in using his abilities and technical skills for purposes connected with his religious beliefs. He focused on learning, creating projects, and using technology as a way to support education and knowledge sharing.

First interest in computers and programming

KossarSherzad's first access to a computer came through his father's computer. He used it as an opportunity to explore technology, learn how computers worked, and understand software systems.

His interest in programming developed from a desire to understand and create technology rather than only use it. He believed that skills and knowledge should be used for meaningful purposes and that technology could become a tool for serving ideas and communities.

Strategic thinking and problem solving

KossarSherzad became known among people around him for his problem solving ability and strategic style of thinking. His interest in strategy was influenced partly by strategy-based games and his interest in history and planning.

During school and later work experiences, friends and colleagues often turned to him when they faced difficult problems. He became recognized among his circle as someone who could analyze situations and search for solutions.

Childhood accident

During his childhood, KossarSherzad experienced a serious accident when he was approximately eight to ten years old. According to his account, he was behind a vehicle when his father reversed the car without knowing he was there, causing him to fall under the vehicle. He survived the incident, which he considers an important event in his life.

Education and learning [edit]

During his education, KossarSherzad continued learning through both school and personal study. At one stage of his education, he was a student in the fifth grade of school.

Alongside formal education, he spent time reading books and exploring different areas of knowledge.

Influences and interests [edit]

Throughout his life, KossarSherzad developed interests in reading, learning, strategy, and understanding different ideas. Books played an important role in shaping his thinking style and his approach to knowledge.

Among the figures who influenced his personal interests was Mulla Krekar , whom KossarSherzad considered an important inspiration. His interest in different thinkers and discussions contributed to his analytical and strategic way of thinking.

These influences, combined with his interest in technology and programming, shaped his approach to creating digital projects and developing systems focused on education and knowledge sharing.

Programming career [edit]

Around 2015, KossarSherzad began learning programming and exploring software development.

He worked with technologies including Java, C#, Firebase, Android development, and web development.

His goal was to use technology to create useful systems and digital platforms.
