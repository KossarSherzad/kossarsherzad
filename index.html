KossarSherzad Sheikh Mohammed, known as KossarSherzad, is a Kurdish developer and technology creator known for developing software projects and creating digital platforms focused on education, technology, and Islamic content.

He is the founder of Darwazay Islam, a digital project created to organize and share Islamic knowledge through modern technology.
